Dracula Simia

by IncredibleNature on September 09, 2026

Dracula simia, also known as the monkey orchid, is an epiphytic orchid. It is known for the unusual appearance of its flowers.

The arrangement of the column, petals, and lip can resemble the face of a monkey. This unusual characteristic has made the orchid famous among plant enthusiasts.

The flowers can appear during different seasons and are known for their pleasant fragrance.
