Datura

by IncredibleNature on September 09, 2026

Datura is a genus of flowering plants belonging to the nightshade family. They are commonly known as thornapples or jimsonweeds.

Datura plants are known for their large and distinctive flowers. Their flowers can have different colors and shapes, making them interesting plants to observe.

Datura species contain toxic compounds and should not be consumed. They are studied for their unusual characteristics and their place in plant diversity.
