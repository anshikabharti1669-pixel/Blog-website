Monotropa Uniflora

by IncredibleNature on September 09, 2026

Monotropa uniflora is commonly known as ghost pipe, ghost plant, or Indian pipe. It is a fascinating non-photosynthesizing flowering plant.

The plant is usually waxy white, although some specimens may have pink or darker coloration. It has a distinctive appearance that makes it easy to recognize.

Monotropa uniflora is associated with fungi in the soil and is commonly found growing in groups in suitable environments.
