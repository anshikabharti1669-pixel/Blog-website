Brugmansia

by IncredibleNature on September 09, 2026

Brugmansia is a genus of flowering plants belonging to the nightshade family Solanaceae. They are woody trees or shrubs with large, hanging flowers.

Their large and fragrant flowers are commonly called angel's trumpets because of their trumpet-like shape.

Brugmansia is related to Datura and is known for its attractive flowers and unusual appearance.
