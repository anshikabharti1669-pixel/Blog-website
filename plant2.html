Amanita Muscaria

by IncredibleNature on September 09, 2026

Amanita muscaria, commonly known as the fly agaric or fly amanita, is a basidiomycete fungus belonging to the genus Amanita.

It is famous for its distinctive appearance. The mushroom commonly has a bright cap with contrasting spots, making it one of the most recognizable mushrooms.

Amanita muscaria contains toxic and psychoactive compounds and should not be consumed. Its unusual appearance makes it an interesting subject in the natural world.
